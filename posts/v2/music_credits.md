# Music credits — v2 videos

All v2 videos use **ambient.wav**, an original track generated on 2026-10-10 by `v2/music.py`
(numpy additive synthesis: 72 BPM, Cmaj9–Am9–Fmaj7–G6 pad progression, sine bass, bell plucks, simple delay).
No samples, loops or third-party recordings are used, so there is no external license; it is our own work and safe for commercial use.
Mastered with ffmpeg loudnorm to -19 LUFS integrated (true peak -2 dBTP), soft so it sits under the visuals; each video is trimmed with 0.8 s fade-in and 1.6 s fade-out.
For more reach, the user can still swap in a sound from TikTok's Commercial Music Library in-app.
